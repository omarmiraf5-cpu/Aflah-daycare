Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Get in touch — we’re always happy to help.

Do we need to be Muslim to enroll?

Not at all. Aflah Daycare welcomes families of every faith, culture and background. Our values of kindness, respect and inclusion are shared by everyone, and faith-based activities are offered with warmth and respect for each family’s wishes.

What ages do you care for?

We care for children from 6 months to 12 years across four programs: Seedlings (infants), Sprouts (toddlers), Saplings (preschool) and Branches (school age).

What are your hours?

We’re open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We’re closed on statutory holidays — please ask us for our full closure calendar.

Are meals provided? Are they halal?

Yes. We provide nutritious, halal meals and snacks each day from a rotating menu. Please let us know about any allergies or dietary needs.

How do you handle allergies and medical needs?

We create an individual care plan for each child with allergies or medical needs, share it with every educator in the room, and plan menus and activities to keep every child safe.

How will I know how my child’s day went?

You’ll receive daily updates on meals, naps and activities, plus photos and regular conversations with your child’s educators. Our door is always open.

Do you offer part-time care?

Depending on availability, we may be able to offer part-time options. Let us know the schedule you need when you join the waitlist.

How does the waitlist work?

Complete the waitlist form with your preferred program and start date. When a space becomes available, we’ll contact you to arrange a tour and next steps.

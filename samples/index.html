Homepage design samples

Three design directions for the Aflah Daycare homepage. Each follows the same layout as the reference site: a photo hero with an “Enroll Now” button, overlapping highlight cards, an about section with a counter badge, program cards, a “Join Us Now” banner, value circles, teachers and “Why choose us”. Pick the one you like and it can be rolled out across every page.

Sample 1

Bright & Playful

Closest to the reference. Clean white and light-grey sections, rainbow lettering from the logo, sunny yellow buttons and lots of photos.

  • Fonts: Poppins
  • Feel: cheerful, familiar, photo-led

Sample 2

Heritage Elegance

Built around the emblem’s navy and gold. Islamic star patterns, arch-shaped photos, an elegant serif and Arabic names for every section.

Sample 3

Soft Pastel

A modern, gentle look. Mint, peach, lavender and sky pastels, blob-shaped photos and illustrated icons, so it looks finished even with few photos.

About the photos

The reference site relies on real photos of children. Until you have yours, the samples use crops of the Aflah logo artwork. Swapping in photos of your centre will make any of these look much closer to the reference.

Mix and match

You can combine ideas, for example the layout and photos of Sample 1 with the navy and gold of Sample 2, or the pastel program cards of Sample 3.

Contact details

The phone number, email and address in the footers are placeholders. They will be replaced with your real details in the final site.

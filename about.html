Our Story

A home away from home

Aflah Daycare was created with one simple hope: that every child who walks through our doors feels happy, safe and truly at home.

We bring together warm, attentive care, play-based early learning and beautiful values, in a space where every family belongs, whatever their background, language or ability.

Our name, Aflah, comes from the Arabic word for flourishing and success. It shares its root with falāḥ, heard in the call to prayer: “Come to success.” For us, success means children who are joyful, curious, kind and confident in who they are.

Aflah means to flourish
and succeed

Our Mission

To provide a nurturing, inclusive and joyful environment where children grow in knowledge, character and confidence, in close partnership with their families.

Our Vision

A community where every child is loved, every family is welcome, and every little seed grows into a flourishing tree.

Our Values

The values that guide us

Six simple words shape how we care, teach and welcome families every day.

Compassion

Raḥmah

We lead with gentleness, patience and warmth in every interaction.

Happiness

Saʿādah

Joyful children learn best, so we fill each day with laughter, play and wonder.

Inclusion

Shumūliyyah

Every child and family belongs here, whatever their background, language or ability.

Respect & manners

Adab

We model kindness, courtesy and respect for each other and for our world.

Excellence

Iḥsān

We give our very best in care, learning and communication, every single day.

Trust

Amānah

Your child is a trust placed in our hands, and we honour it with care and safety.

Our Teachers

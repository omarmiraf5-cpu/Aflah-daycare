About Aflah Daycare

The right environment for growing

We provide a nurturing, safe and engaging environment where children explore at their own pace. Our educators support every child’s growth with patience, respect and care, and our doors are open to families of every background.

Our name, Aflah, means “to flourish and succeed”, and that’s exactly what we wish for every child.

Our Value

A Brighter Future through Play

We believe play is the foundation of learning. Through art, music, movement, storytelling and gentle Islamic values, children discover important skills: creativity, collaboration, problem-solving, kindness and self-confidence.

  • Play-based, child-led learning
  • Arabic letters, duas and good manners (adab)
  • Every family welcome, whatever their background
